This is an interactive exploration of the parts of a bicycle.

If you're reading this message, the images may still be loading.

If you are using a screen reader or have Javascript disabled, unfortunately you won't be able to see the images or animations of the parts, but you can still read the descriptions here.

Thank you for visiting!

You can use still this site on a mobile device, but it won't be as sharp for a few reasons:

  • Bikes fit better in a landscape orientation and I haven't had time to build a portrait orientation of this site!
  • Many of the bike parts are quite small and harder to click with fat fingers than they are with a mouse.
  • You can't hover over items to see what they're called.

Sorry about that! If you have some time, check it out on a bigger screen.
